What changed under the hood

behaviour untouched · feel rebuilt
  • Boss art

    All three Cerberus phases wired to their real sprites — no more grey placeholder blocks when the boss changes phase.

  • Aliveness

    Boss breathes, winds up, and recoils. A rising stretch before each attack doubles as a free telegraph. Applies to all 21 bosses.

  • Projectiles

    Every shot is an oriented comet — glow, motion-tail and hot core — instead of a flat circle. Homing, sonic and creep each read differently.

  • Swings

    The melee arc now sweeps and trails like a real blade, and shots spark when they hit a wall.

  • Enemies

    Grubs, imps and brutes redrawn as little pixel creatures — original art, no AI.
